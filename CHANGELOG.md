# Changelog

## v1.0.0 — first release

**Download:** `KittenPlanner-1.0.0-portable.exe` below — a portable app, no installation needed.
Windows SmartScreen may ask once because the build is not code-signed (_More info → Run anyway_).

### Highlights

- **Launch roadmaps** for opening a bookbinding workshop & souvenir shop in Coburg: 23 legal steps
  (Gewerbeanmeldung, Finanzamt, Kleinunternehmer rule, HWK, lease, TSE, GPSR, online-shop duties…) and
  17 commercial steps, each with checklists, links, due dates and notes.
- **Workshop configurator** for notebooks, pocket notebooks, notepads, photo albums, blank books and guest books
  with live preview, material costs, labour, markup, VAT / § 19 UStG and a suggested retail price; product catalog.
- **Finance**: income/expenses, monthly chart, simplified EÜR, Kleinunternehmer limit tracker, CSV export,
  German invoices (§ 14 UStG) as PDF.
- **Documents** archive with deadlines; **plans**, **calendar** and **notes**.
- **Painting of the day**: 479 famous paintings from Old Masters to early modernism, one hidden behind every
  calendar day from 16 October 2026 to the end of 2027, with a bilingual story about each work. Images are bundled.
- **AI tab**: sign in to your own ChatGPT account inside the app, or use an OpenAI API key; the little robot
  **Auerilus** plays tag with the kitten next to the chat.
- **3D kitten** that follows the cursor, jumps onto the button you hover, naps when the mouse rests and purrs
  when petted — plus a **pet corner** with two more cats and Bublyk the corgi.
- Ukrainian UI with a German switch, light and dark theme; all data stays on the local machine.
